# Story Bank — Master STAR+R Stories

This file accumulates your best interview stories over time. Each evaluation (Block F) adds new stories here. Instead of memorizing 100 answers, maintain 5-10 deep stories that you can bend to answer almost any behavioral question.

## How it works

1. Every time `/career-ops oferta` generates Block F (Interview Plan), new STAR+R stories get appended here
2. Before your next interview, review this file — your stories are already organized by theme
3. The "Big Three" questions can be answered with stories from this bank:
   - "Tell me about yourself" → combine 2-3 stories into a narrative
   - "Tell me about your most impactful project" → pick your highest-impact story
   - "Tell me about a conflict you resolved" → find a story with a Reflection

### Fraud Analytics Pipeline and Real-Time Processing
**Source:** Monash University — Big Data & Real-Time Fraud Analytics Project

**S (Situation):**  
I worked on a large-scale fraud analytics project involving over 5 million e-commerce transaction records. The project focused on detecting suspicious transaction behaviour using scalable batch and streaming workflows.

**T (Task):**  
My responsibility was to support the design and implementation of data pipelines using PySpark, Spark SQL, and Kafka while ensuring the workflows remained scalable and efficient.

**A (Action):**  
I engineered behavioural features such as browsing patterns, time-of-day activity, and customer demographic metrics to improve fraud detection analysis. I also worked with Spark Structured Streaming concepts to process transactions in near real time and focused on improving the reliability and consistency of the data pipeline.

**R (Result):**  
The project successfully demonstrated how scalable analytics systems can process large datasets efficiently while supporting low-latency fraud monitoring workflows.

**Reflection:**  
This project taught me that strong analytics depends heavily on good data engineering foundations. Building scalable pipelines is just as important as building the final model itself.

**Best for questions about:**  
Big data, PySpark, Kafka, data pipelines, scalability, technical problem solving, handling large datasets.
